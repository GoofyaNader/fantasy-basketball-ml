How it works

A gradient-boosted model trained on five NBA seasons of box scores, with no information from after each game's tip-off.

  • Recent form

    Fantasy points and minutes over the last 3, 5 and 10 games, season and last-season averages, efficiency and consistency.

  • Game context

    Rest days, back-to-backs, home or away, and how many fantasy points the opponent usually allows.

  • Teammate absences

    When a rotation teammate sits, the others pick up his shots. The model learned this from games where regulars were missing.

  • Injury report

    ESPN's live report sets each player's chance of playing each game, and feeds the teammate-absence features.

How the draft model works

Two gradient-boosted models trained on nine NBA seasons, one row per player per season, using only what was known before each season started.

  • Points per game

    From the last three seasons' scoring, minutes, efficiency, usage, and form over the final 20 games of last season.

  • Games played

    Availability matters as much as talent. The model learns who tends to miss time from each player's recent seasons and age.

  • Age

    Young players tend to improve and veterans tend to decline. The model learns the shape of that curve from the data.

  • Current injuries

    Players out long-term lose the games their team plays before their expected return date.
